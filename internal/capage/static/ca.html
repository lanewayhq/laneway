Laneway

Trust Laneway on this device

Laneway's Worktrees, Dashboard and Service Domains use certificates from its own certificate authority, on the PC it runs on. Install that authority's root certificate once on each of your devices, and their browsers open every Domain without a warning.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari, tap Download the profile, then Allow.
  2. Open Settings, tap Profile Downloaded near the top (or General → VPN & Device Management), tap Laneway, then Install.
  3. Go to Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and turn on full trust for {{.Name}}.

Android

  1. Tap Download the certificate.
  2. Open Settings and search for CA certificate. On most phones it's under Security & privacy → More security settings → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate → CA certificate.
  3. Tap Install anyway and pick laneway-root.crt from your downloads.

Chrome and most other browsers then trust it.

Firefox on Android

Firefox keeps its own list, so install the certificate as above, then:

  1. In Firefox, go to Settings → About Firefox and tap the Firefox logo until it says the debug menu is on.
  2. Back in Settings, open Secret Settings and turn on Use third party CA certificates.

Only the public root certificate is offered here: its private key never leaves the PC. Install it only on devices you own, since a device that trusts it trusts every certificate Laneway issues.
